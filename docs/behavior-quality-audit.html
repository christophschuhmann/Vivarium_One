Behavior and UI audit · 8 October 2026

Reviewed actual journals, not only test return codes. Compared the same seeded 100-resident town over 72 hours before and after changes; sampled two residents in each of seven age cohorts. Separately advanced a SQLite backup of the existing 1,000-resident Bennington world by 24 hours. The live world was not advanced.

Outcome: concrete causal defects were repaired; this does not establish that every earlier design idea is fully implemented or that all future stories will be plausible.

Findings and repairs

AreaObserved problemChange
Romantic need saturationConstant accumulation made the average adult need 99.7% after three days and dominated 79 of 100 primary moods.Individual slow mean reversion; sleep does not accumulate longing. Supportive partner contact has a small, separate effect. Age, kinship and consent gates remain independent.
Warmth after conflictEvery accepted exchange, including arguments, subtracted 18 points from unmet social warmth.Needs, emotions and PERMA use the same outcome classification. Arguments and dismissive responses can hurt; setting a boundary is not automatically hostility or affection.
Disposable relationship dramaA hurtful response could disappear from decisions after the short emotion expired.Bounded, personal unfinished-conversation records retain the real source event. Measured checks

The 100-resident replay finished all 4,320 minute ticks with no detected repeated interruption/restart loops. Adult romantic need at the final snapshot averaged 27.6% (previously 99.7%). This is a balance check, not a target everyone should converge toward. No resident remained blocked by unavailable facilities in either final run.

The copied 1,000-resident world completed 1,440 ticks in 220.1 seconds of wall time while other verification was running on the same host. It retained 368 personal unresolved relationship impressions. One hour in 60 one-minute calls produces the same causal state as one 60-minute call. Fifteen regression suites cover economy, education/care, age/consent, social consequences, private perspectives, providers and storage.

Browser review covers actual asset loading, large-neighborhood expansion, the 50-thumbnail budget, scene jumps, relationship refocusing, keyboard/pointer navigation, mobile layout, visible age/job/needs/attributes, and English concept cards. Hybrid conversations, intervention, TTS autoplay and music are exercised with controlled fixtures; no new paid narrative quality evaluation was performed.

Limits that still matter

  • Game heuristics, not a validated psychological or demographic model.
  • No new paid LLM narration quality evaluation in this audit; hybrid integration uses controlled model responses.
  • Multi-day sample review does not prove year-long plausibility.
  • Detailed history storage is still large; approximately 850 MiB growth in the copied 1000-resident database over a day, including every event, personal journal and reversible state differences.
  • 600 taxonomy examples are contextual variants, not 600 independently implemented actions.
  • Many occupations share base activities and schedules. More shift and travel diversity remains useful.

The copied database reached 896.8 MiB including its prior worlds. History compaction/archive work is needed before treating month- or year-long city-scale runs as practical. No history was deleted to improve this measurement.
